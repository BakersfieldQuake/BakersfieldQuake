# BakersfieldQuake 6-Kart, Separate Driver & Skid/Peel-Out Handoff

All **6 procedural 64-frame MD3 go-karts** (`gokart_1..6.md3`) now spawn with **empty cockpits when parked**, attach the standalone **1996 Quake Ranger driver** (`progs/gokart_driver.md3`) only while a player is driving, and support **peel-outs, natural high-speed skidding/sliding, and thick rear-tire smoke** in QuakeC (`gokart.qc`) and the live WebGL2 showcase.

### B. QuakeC Driving, Driver Attachment & Skid/Peel-Out Physics (`source/qc_combat_decomp/` & `effectinfo.txt`)
* **Compiled Output:** `progs.dat` and `progs.lno` (compiled in `source/qc_combat_decomp/` with `fteqcc64.exe -srcfile progs.src`)
* **Driver Entity Attachment (`v.veh_driverent`):**
  * `source/qc_combat_decomp/vehicles_defs.qc`: defines `.entity veh_driverent;`, `.float gk_smoke_t;`, and `float gk_fx_smoke;`
  * `source/qc_combat_decomp/vehicles.qc`: precaches `progs/gokart_driver.md3` and `gk_fx_smoke = particleeffectnum ("gk_tire_smoke");`, and removes `veh_driverent` on `Veh_ExplodeNow`.
  * `source/qc_combat_decomp/vehicle_drive.qc`: `Veh_Enter` spawns `v.veh_driverent` only for `Veh_IsKart`; `Veh_Leave` removes `v.veh_driverent` when exiting so parked karts stay empty.
  * `source/qc_combat_decomp/gokart.qc`: `GK_SyncDriverEnt` keeps `v.veh_driverent` synced to kart `origin`, `angles`, and `frame` (hidden via `EF_NODRAW` only in 1st-person cockpit view `veh_camera == 0`).
* **Peel-Outs, High-Speed Skids & Tire Smoke:**
  * `effectinfo.txt`: `effect gk_tire_smoke` (bright, thick white/light-grey rubber smoke clouds behind rear tires).
  * `source/qc_combat_decomp/gokart.qc`:
    * Computes momentum-preserving lateral velocity `vl1` after heading change (`vel_world` projected onto new `(fwd1, lft1)`).
    * Speed- and steering-dependent rear lateral grip (`lat_grip`) breaks traction smoothly above `520 u/s` on sharp turns (`fabs(veh_steer) > 0.38`), braking+steering (`brk > 0.15`), or Nitro turns — allowing natural high-speed slides even without holding `SPACE` (while `SPACE` still locks 3-tier Mini-Turbo powerslides).
    * `GK_PeelAndSkidFX` emits `gk_tire_smoke` + `veh_drift` sparks + pitch-scaled `vehicles/tire_squeal.wav` on launch peel-outs, burnouts, high-speed slides, and drifts.

### C. Python Asset & Showcase Pipeline (`source/mm_tools/`)
* `source/mm_tools/gokart_driver.py` — extracts Ranger body from `player_id1.mdl`, poses seated posture, builds `progs/gokart_driver.md3` + `progs/gk_driver.png`.
* `source/mm_tools/gokart_1.py` .. `source/mm_tools/gokart_6.py` — empty 64-frame MD3 kart builders + texture generators.
* `source/mm_tools/gokart_showcase.template.html` & `source/mm_tools/build_gokart_showcase.py` — packs empty karts + `gokart_driver.md3` (`Seated Driver (Ranger)` toggle + `Skid / Peel-Out!` mode) and deploys to VPS.

## 3. Shared 64-Frame Animation Contract (`0..63`)

* `0..11` (`idle_00..11`): idle rumble, tires at `Z = 0`
* `12..27` (`fast_00..15`): wheel spin (`2π` or `4π`) + suspension chatter
* `28..43` (`super_00..15`): `2×` wheel spin, rear squat / nose lift, rear MD3 `flame_outer` / `flame_core` plumes
* `44..63` (`explode_00..19`): fireball + tumbling ballistic dispersal (`Z >= 0.5` bounce)
