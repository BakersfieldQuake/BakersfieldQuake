Section 04 · Material & Lighting Fixes

Vehicle Visual Fixes: bklan6 Yellow Blowout & Non-Pink Tuk Tuk

Eliminated warm-lighting vehicle yellow saturation in 6 Band Land and stopped map/traffic colormod tinting from turning painted Thai Tuk Tuks solid pink.

Verified in Zircon

bklan6 ("6 Band Land") Yellow Lighting Fix

  • Root cause: bklan6 uses warm bright lighting (_sunlight_color "1 0.94 0.78", _minlight 42, _minlight_color "255 245 228"), which blew out bright vans, sedans, forklifts, and school buses into yellow when gl_overbright_models was 1.
  • Fix: enforced seta gl_overbright_models "0" (paired with seta r_minlight "3") across default.cfg, graphics.cfg, bm_up_11.cfg, and the MenuQC Graphics tab.
  • Verified: bklan6 vehicles render with natural white, red, blue, and silver paint.

Non-Pink Tuk Tuk (traffic_cars.qc, vehicles.qc)

  • Root cause: progs/veh_tuktuk.md3 already ships a full painted livery (progs/veh_tuktuk_tex.png), so Traffic_AutoColor ('1.08 0.38 0.65') and map colormod ("1.75 0.45 0.95" on bakersfielddowntown) tinted the entire hull solid pink.
  • Fix: traffic_cars.qc forces self.colormod = '1 1 1' when kind == TC_TUKTUK; vehicles.qc preserves neutral '0 0 0' veh_orig_colormod for VEH_TUKTUK.
  • Verified: both parked misc_vehicle_tuktuk and moving misc_traffic_car cnt=5 render natural blue/red/white Thai Tuk Tuk artwork.
