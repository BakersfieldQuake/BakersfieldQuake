Empty Parked Karts + Separate Driver & Skid Physics

  • Empty Cockpits When Parked: progs/gokart_{1..6}.md3 ship with empty seats so approaching a parked go-kart never shows a phantom driver inside.
  • Attached Quake Ranger While Driven: progs/gokart_driver.md3 (218v / 294t, progs/gk_driver.png) spawns onto v.veh_driverent only when a player enters (Veh_Enter) and is removed on exit or explosion (Veh_Leave).
  • High-Speed Skids, Peel-Outs & Tire Smoke: gokart.qc computes momentum-based lateral tire slip + speed-dependent rear grip so hard high-speed turns, braking turns, and launches peel out & slide naturally with gk_tire_smoke clouds + tire_squeal.wav.
