Mystery Box — who comes out?

Throw the rainbow box, it lands and bursts into confetti, and two random monsters rush the nearest enemy, jump on it, bounce around doing damage and explode. Switch on the characters you want in the pool (all 16 are on to start). If you already know what one should do, write it in its note. The game reads the result from the cvar mb_pool.
