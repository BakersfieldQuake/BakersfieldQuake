Mystery Box — who comes out, and what do they sound like?

The rainbow box bursts into confetti and one random monster runs flat out at the nearest enemy, stands in its face, hits it a few times and explodes. There are far more characters now, so this page does both votes in one go. Step 1: switch on every monster that may come out of the box (the pool). Step 2: click a monster that is in the box and give it two sounds of its own: what it sounds like while it runs (a loop) and what it sounds like when it explodes. Press Play on a sound, then choose My pick (tick Also fine on runners-up). Sounds made for a monster come first, but any sound in the game can go to any monster: open Pick from ALL sounds to search every file in the mod's sound folders and the stock Quake sounds. If you already know what a monster should do, write it in its note. Sound only starts when you press a button, and your choices are kept in this browser.
