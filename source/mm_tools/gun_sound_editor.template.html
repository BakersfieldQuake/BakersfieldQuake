Gun sound editor

Every sound the custom guns make. Pick a gun, press Play on a shot to hear the whole thing in order, or the round button on one card to hear just that sound. The orange name is what the game plays now. Change sound opens everything you can swap it for, with a play button on each. Your picks save in this browser as you go. When you are done press Download changes (or Copy) and tell Claude: nothing in the game changes until then.
