# Part B: S Union Ave landmarks, camera positions 11-20 (images 101-180)

Method: viewed only the yaw 0/90/180/270 images (odd n). Cameras sit on the NORTHBOUND (east) carriageway.
Union Ave here is a divided road: ~3 lanes each way, red-brick-paved raised median (with palms, shrubs, tall
grey cobra-head double-arm street lights), yellow edge line on the median side, painted green bike lane + "BUS"
pavement text on the kerb lane, wooden utility poles with overhead lines along both frontages, tall palms
(Mexican fan palms) along the median and frontages. Text I could not read is marked "unreadable".
In yaw 0 (north) shots, left = west, right = east. In yaw 180 (south) shots, left = east, right = west.
The camera order south to north follows the gallery numbering: Lloyd, Wilkins, E 1st/110 Union, Marcus, Texas,
E 3rd, Bill, E 4th, Econo lot, E 5th. (Wilkins/E 1st/Marcus views repeat the same Parkhouse Tire block, so
positions are close together.) Captions' "KEY" notes do not always match the image number; the notes below are
what the image actually shows.
