# Part D - S Union Ave landmarks, camera positions 31-41 (images 261-348)

Source: golden_west_gallery, yaw 0 (N), 90 (E), 180 (S), 270 (W) images only (odd file numbers). Route runs south to north.
Conventions: "W side" / "E side" = west / east of Union Ave. Sizes are eyeball estimates. Colours as seen in the Street View crop (lighting shifts them).
Imagery dates differ: Eureka St (293-299) is 2021 imagery, 17th St / 18th / 20th / 21st / destination are 2026, 19th St (317-323) is 2025. Businesses may differ between dates.
Order of positions going north: Chico St, Truxtun underpass, Truxtun frontage road, 17th, Eureka, 18th, vacant lot (E, off-road), 19th, 20th, 21st, destination (on 21st).

KEY TAKEAWAYS (read this first)
- Union Ave from Chico St to just north of Truxtun runs in a DEPRESSED CUT: tall poured-concrete retaining walls on both sides, the CA-99 BUS / Truxtun Ave bridge crosses over it, a narrow service "frontage" road lies up on the east side.
- Union Ave from 17th north is a divided 4-lane road (2 each way) with a raised median faced in red brick-coloured paving (planted with palms / shrubs in places), green-painted bike lanes on the curbs, grey mast-arm signals with black heads, grey cobra-head streetlights, wood utility poles with overhead lines on one side.
- Corner pattern 17th-19th: McDonald's (E side, between 18th and 19th, golden-arch pole sign at 19th), Mobil gas station (W side, between 18th and 19th), 7-Eleven + fuel canopy (NE corner of 19th), Salvation Army Family Store (W side, N of 19th).
- 20th-21st: Motels on E side (brown wood siding / tan stucco) and a blue "Automotive" garage + billboard; GCI-style equipment yard pole sign at NE of 21st; Capri Motel pylon at SE of 21st St.
- Destination block: low flat-roof white commercial strip at 118-120 E 21st St (north side of 21st) with blue/red panel accents; decorative breeze-block (concrete screen) wall at 121 E 21st on the south side.
