# Golden West route, landmarks part A: camera positions 1-10 (images 21-100)

Method: only the yaw 0 / 90 / 180 / 270 images were viewed (files 021, 023, 025, 027, 029 ... 099, every second file). Crops of 023, 027, 069, 083 and 089 were enlarged to read signs. Anything marked "unreadable" could not be read at the 800x532 crop size.

General notes for the whole range
- Union Ave here is a wide arterial: roughly 3 travel lanes each way (4 at Brundage) plus a left-turn lane, with a continuous median. Median is a **red/terracotta brick-paver strip with a concrete curb**. It is narrow between Adams and Trinity and wider at Belle Terrace and south of the SR-58 area. The yellow double stripe sits on the median-side edge.
- Every pano is shot from the **west half of the road** (median on the right when facing north, on the left when facing south).
- Street lights: galvanized or white steel poles with curved cobra-head arms; double-arm heads on the median. Overhead: wood utility poles with crossarms and transformers (both sides), plus lattice cell towers (positions 5, 6, 7).
- Signals (positions 5, 8, 10): black 3-section heads on galvanized steel mast arms, with green street-name blades.
- Image epochs differ. Positions 1-4 are about 2024 imagery (dry, green leaves, harsh sun). Positions 5-8 and 10 are 2025-26 (positions 5-7 in winter: bare deciduous trees, overcast). Position 9 is 2022. So the Circle K and the red-roofed businesses are the newest.
- Convention used below: left or right is relative to the viewing direction, with the real side (W/E) in brackets.
