# Part C - S Union Ave landmarks, camera positions 21-30 (images 181-260)

Only yaw 0 / 90 / 180 / 270 images viewed (odd file numbers). Sizes are eyeballed from Street View, so treat them as approximate.
"West/east" is relative to Union Ave (yaw 0 = north, 90 = east, 180 = south, 270 = west).

## Corridor-wide look (applies to every position here)
- Roadway: about 3 lanes each way plus a left-turn lane at signals, with a raised median. It is about 100-110 ft curb to curb, wide and flat.
- The median is paved in **red/terracotta brick pavers** with a brick-edged curb. Pavers also pave the sidewalk strip beside it. Median shrubs are low rounded evergreen bushes.
- Bike lanes are marked with **bright lime-green paint squares/strips** on the outer lanes (shown from about 8th St up to the 15th St underpass).
- **Tall Mexican fan palms** (about 50-70 ft, thin trunks) line both sidewalks and the median, in rows.
- Utility poles: wooden, with 2-3 crossarms, on the east side. Streetlights are grey galvanized single-arm cobra heads. Traffic signals hang from long grey mast arms, black heads, with green street-name blades.
- Brand-new sidewalks and low curbs. Wire fences are mostly black wrought-iron picket or chain link.
- Sky is wide and open, with mountains visible at the horizon on the west and north.
