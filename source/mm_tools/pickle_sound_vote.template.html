Pickle Surprise: pick the sounds

Every clip from the Pickle Surprise audio, cut into short pieces. Play them and mark the ones that should be a sound in the game. A clip can be more than one thing. The star is for "this is just funny", whatever it ends up as. The words under each clip are a machine guess, so trust your ears. P1 and P2 are the two whole "Pickle surprise" lines (P1 is what the game uses now). Votes save in your browser as you go; use Download or Copy when you are done.
