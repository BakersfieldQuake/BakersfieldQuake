Pick the hand for THE DISK

The NetSend gun's old 3D hand is replaced by a sprite built from your seven Grok videos, the same way the Milky Gun's hand is made: every frame keyed off the white backdrop and put on one card. There are three animation sets to compare. Each has a rest hold, trick idles that play by themselves, and a throw. Watch them below, step through any clip frame by frame, then vote.

01Compare the three sets

Each screen runs the same logic the game will run: the hand rests and breathes, every few seconds it does one of its tricks, and fire throws. The thrown disk is the real projectile model, flying from the same spot with the same speed and gravity as in the game. Click a screen or press Space to throw. 1–3 plays a trick.

02Frame inspector

Every clip of every set, frame by frame, on a checkerboard so you can judge the keying: no white halos, no holes, no jumps. The throw clips are at 60 fps; the release frame is marked.

03The thrown disk

The projectile (progs/netsend_disk.md3) is rebuilt to match the disk in the hand: a blue 3.5" floppy textured from your own video frames. The front comes from v1 f240 and the back from v2 f90, each rectified with the glove painted out. It has rounded corners, the angled corner notch and a raised metal shutter. Drag to turn it.

04Source videos: what went where

All seven clips (24 fps, 241 frames each) were split into frames and keyed. The coloured bars show which frames each set uses. Grey marks material that was looked at and left out, with the reason.
