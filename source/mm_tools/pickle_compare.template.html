Pickle Surprise: every model, side by side

Each model is shown with the texture the game gives it, animated like the game does. Drag to turn one. The blue 16 VIEWS pickle is the new standing balloon: it always turns to face you and swaps to the picture of the pickle seen from where you stand, so orbiting it shows the front, the sides and the back. The old single card is thin and vanishes edge-on.

Sizes are in Quake units at scale 1 (a player is 56 tall). In the game the standing pickle is scaled up to 5.7 and the thrown one to 1.7. A model with no thickness in any direction is a flat card: its red FLAT tag is worked out from the model itself, not typed in.
