Laptop Gun: pick the sounds

Three kinds of clips. Canis Lupus pieces are the audio from canisdur.wmv cut into short parts (C0 is the whole thing, which is what plays while the head flies now). Made sounds are keyboard, modem, zap and crash sounds I synthesized, several takes for each job; the ones marked "in the game now" are what you hear today. Your cuts are parts of the Canis clip that you choose yourself with the waveform below.

Play a clip, then press the job it should do. A clip can do more than one job. The star means "this is just funny". Votes and your cuts save in your browser as you go; press Download or Copy when you are done and send me the file.

Cut your own part of the Canis Lupus clip

Drag across the waveform to choose a part, drag its edges to fine-tune, or type exact seconds. Play it, then save it as your clip and give it a job below.
