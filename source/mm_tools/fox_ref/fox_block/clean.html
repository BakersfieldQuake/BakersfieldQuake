Gaps and limits — read before building

  • Wall St at H St: no official Google car pano exists on Wall St itself at H. The only imagery there is a user photosphere (Johnny Johnston, Jul 2026), and Google's thumbnail endpoint will not serve it — all 8 download attempts failed. The nearest official pano (H St just north of Wall) is included instead.
  • Bakersfield Music Theatre (1931 Chester Ave): no direct 20th St pano and no legible name sign in the nearest pano — it shows the high-school auditorium block's plain white stucco wall only. The theatre's position on the block is known from its address; its facade detail is not in this file.
  • The 18hundred (1800 Chester Ave): no legible 18hundred sign in either pano (Chester facade Feb 2026 or 18th St side May 2024). The building's ground-floor tenants are legible instead.
  • Fox Theater: the auditorium 360 photo (Mar 2019) and the Deen Foxx Feb 2017 panos (entrance doors, under-marquee ticket booth) could not be downloaded — Google returns an identical 3910-byte black placeholder JPEG for all contributor panos. The Threshold 360 rendered views (exterior + mezzanine lobby interior) are included instead.
  • Orientation: the Fox Theater is on the WEST side of H St and its facade faces EAST (look WEST from H St). The block is bounded by 20th St (north), 19th St (south), H St (west), Eye St (east); the theater's rear wall is on Service St, the mid-block alley between 20th and 19th. Wall St is ~50 m SOUTH of 19th St, not the north side.
  • Marquee text (Feb 2026): Fox side face BABY BASH MC / MAGIC LIL ROB / FEB 15 and CARLOS MENCIA / FEB 20; south face FOX / ALI SIDDIQ / IN THE SHADOWS TOUR / FEB 14; tower red vertical FOX, blue dome top. Nile marquee: SPLOONKY RAVE 2.6 / LOS HERMANOS MENDOZA 2.15 / CUP CAKKE SOLD OUT 2.21.

Rebuild the Fox Theater block in downtown Bakersfield, CA, from the reference photos in this file. The block is bounded by 21st St (north), 18th St (south), G St (west) and K St (east). Key buildings, in order of importance:
1. The Historic Bakersfield Fox Theater, 2001 H St — on the WEST side of H St, facade faces EAST (look WEST from H St). Art-deco facade, marquee over the sidewalk on H St, red vertical FOX tower sign, blue dome top. Rear wall on Service St (mid-block alley between 20th and 19th St). Marquee text Feb 2026: BABY BASH MC / MAGIC LIL ROB / FEB 15, CARLOS MENCIA / FEB 20, ALI SIDDIQ / IN THE SHADOWS TOUR / FEB 14.
2. The Padre Hotel, 1702 18th St — 8-storey cream tower at 18th & H, THE PADRE canopy, rooftop PADRE HOTEL sign, vertical PADRE HOTEL sign on the H St side.
3. The Nile Theater, 1721 19th St — marquee with Nile on both sides, red/blue/white chevron trim, blue tile base.
4. The Ovation Theatre, 1622 19th St — two-storey, five blue awnings, OVATION window sign.
5. Buena Vista Museum of Natural History & Science, 2018 Chester Ave — green-and-yellow upper facade, blue sign, blue gate with vertical MUSEUM and 2018, animal silhouettes.
6. The Woolworth's Luncheonette, 1400 19th St — red curved F. W. WOOLWORTH CO. sign, green awning.
7. Dagny's Coffee, 1600 20th St — two-storey white curved-corner building, orange/red awning (its own sign is NOT legible in the May 2024 pano).
8. The Mint Bar, 1207 19th St — COCKTAILS / The MINT green script sign.
9. The 18hundred, 1800 Chester Ave — no legible name sign in the photos; ground-floor tenants are an Oaxaqueno restaurant, a Brazilian acai bar, Habibi's Hookah and a smoke shop.
10. Today Cleaners, 2033 H St — TODAY CLEANERS / IN BY 10 READY AT 4.
Streets: 18th, 19th, 20th, 21st run east-west; G, H, Eye, Chester, K run north-south. Service St and Wall St are mid-block east-west alleys (Service between 20th and 19th; Wall just south of 19th). 20th Pl is a small street between 20th and 21st, Eye to Chester. Use the aerials for the block layout and rooflines, the 8-direction Street View shots for each facade, and the yaw labels (N/E/S/W) for orientation. Do not invent signage that is not legible in the photos — where a sign is not legible, the file says so.
