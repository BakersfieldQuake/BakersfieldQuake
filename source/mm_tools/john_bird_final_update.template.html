1. QuakeC Mechanics (john_bird_defs.qc, john_bird.qc, skypad.qc)

  • Fast Flight (fast_fly, 100..115): Triggers when hspd >= 950 u/s (Shift boost 1,280 u/s or mouse-carve up to 1,220 u/s) and not steep-diving (velocity_z >= -420). Scales 22..32 fps with 0.14..0.24s flap SFX at 108..126% pitch.
  • Hit / Disoriented (hit_flail, 116..131): JB_Pain sets jb_hurt_until = time + 0.65 + jb_hurt_skin_until = time + 0.18 (skin = 1 hurt-wing flash), light roll/velocity jolt, pilot punch, 8× john_bird_feather.md3, and feather_hit.wav. Plays at 28 fps with 0.11s erratic flaps in both flight & perch idle.
  • Super Launch Pad (skypad_hyper, 132..147): SkyPad_Touch + 20 Hz JB_CheckSkyPads call SkyPad_LaunchJohnBird → SkyPad_CalcVel, raise sv_maxvelocity 6500, play skypad/launch.wav, preserve ballistic arc (gravity 800 + light air-steering without cruise clamp), shed continuous 0.06s feather trail, and play at 30..42 fps while jb_skypad_until > time || hspd >= 1500.
