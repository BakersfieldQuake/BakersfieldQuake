## Overall Verdict: **FAIL**

The visual foundation of `bakersfieldish.bsp` is strong — H Street faithfully preserves `bakersfielddowntown`'s scale and iconic **Padre Hotel ↔ Bakersfield Sign ↔ Fox Theater + Parking Garage** corridor, the skybox is genuinely high (`Z = 7211` vs `3371` in downtown), the warm golden-hour haze and light-handed rubble/barriers hit the intended post-apocalyptic tone without becoming a wasteland, **Fox Theater** completes its full organic collapse → rubble → auto-rebuild → restore cycle, **The Bakersfield Sign** topples into rampable wreckage, **shatterable glass** works crisply across all 5 tested buildings, **Padre Hotel** takes real damage from rockets and Eric Cannon at 13× Fox's HP with climbable rubble and zero floating rooftop items, and **5 of 6** chained rooftop `info_skypad` hops auto-chain seamlessly across the sky.

However, live Zircon execution exposed **5 critical gameplay/map blockers** (plus 5 moderate visual/clipping issues) that require fixes before ship:

1. **[CRITICAL] Deathmatch spawn instantly telefrags & collapses Padre Hotel on frame 1** (50,000 telefrag damage from `spawn_tdeath` touching `bako_padre_piece`).
2. **[CRITICAL] Golden West Casino rooftop `info_skypad #4`, spawn `#19`, and `misc_gokart_2` are buried inside a sealed attic** below the `Z=241..373` outer roof, breaking the 6-pad cross-map SkyPad chain dead at Golden West Casino.
3. **[CRITICAL] Padre Hotel rolling/flying boulders fail to bounce players, bots, or NPCs** (`0` bounces across 4 live collapses / 96 boulders due to `MOVETYPE_BOUNCE + SOLID_TRIGGER` and missing player/monster checks in `Bako_BoulderThink`).
4. **[CRITICAL] Parking Garage L6 `misc_eric_cannon` (`-7404 -5520 654`) is overlapped 12u away by a `misc_gokart`**, clipping both models together and causing `H` (`impulse 86`) to board the kart instead of mounting the Padre-facing cannon.
5. **[CRITICAL] 3 startup `droptofloor` failures + 3 runtime Fox rebuild `npc_bako_worker` `droptofloor` failures** (violating the `0 droptofloor failures` requirement).
