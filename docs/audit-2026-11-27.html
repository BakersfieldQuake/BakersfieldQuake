BakersfieldQuake audit

27 November 2026. Three passes: bugs, broken mechanics, and optimization without changing how the mod plays.

Summary

  • 7 bugs and 5 broken mechanics found and fixed, plus 5 optimizations.
  • Commits f79c69f4 (fixes) and 4f6e7dda (optimizations). First released in v1.102; also in v1.113.
  • Only 2 of the 12 fixes had a targeted in-game check. The rest rest on reading the code, compiling, and general play runs.
  • A large part of the weapon, map, NPC and menu code was not read line by line.

Performance findings

  • Server game code was not what limited frame rate: about 2,300 script statements per player per frame.
  • With 3 bots, start and bakersfielddowntown held the 250 fps cap. The only frames under 60 fps were first-use shader compiles. Worst frames in the final runs were 24 ms and 34 ms.
  • In the client profile, the minimap was about 70% of client script statements (BM_DrawMinimap). I did not change it. The minimap has been reworked since, so this needs measuring again.

What was run

  • All server, client and menu code compiled with the CI-pinned compiler.
  • 17 weapons selected, fired and reloaded.
  • Kart spawn, drive, exit, death in the kart and respawn.
  • Time limit to intermission to next map, three times.
  • Bot matches on start and bakersfielddowntown, with no script errors.

Harness and logs: G:\Quake\Combat\audit_reports\2026-11-27 (on this PC, not in the repo). All runs predate the commits other sessions added afterwards.

Open leads, not confirmed and not changed

  • A driver may be left below the map after a vehicle explodes out of the world.
  • A driver netted by Cyrax may freeze their vehicle.
  • A spawn may telefrag someone on a driven kart.
  • The scoreboard may stick if TAB is released while the console is open.
  • Bot fill and idle cleanup may fight on a public server when bm_botfill_empty 1 is set (a test setting).

Not reviewed

These ran in the tests at most, and were not read line by line: most fun guns (Mega Man, Pickle, green jar, Dookie, Kyle, Rob head, NetSend, Wheelman, M4, Garand, Milky, Cyrax, Eric cannon, tank), destructibles, NPCs, hats, minimap, bot internals, the four HUD styles and most menus. The parallel reviewers meant to cover them ran into the usage limit.

Suggested next step

Test the ten fixes marked "Code only" in game, starting with the flashlight and the one-shot jump pads, since those could affect unrelated entities.
